Privacy

IPV Viewer is a web page that runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own and doesn't collect, store, or share your data.

Files you open from your computer

They're read in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.

Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, the page asks only for access to the files you choose (or open with IPV Viewer from Drive). For each file it downloads the file into your browser, reads the finished artwork from it, and saves that image back to the same file as its Drive thumbnail. Nothing else in your Drive is read or changed.

Google's sign-in gives the page a temporary access token, which is kept only in the open browser tab and is gone when you close it. You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings.

The page's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
